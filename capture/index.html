Capture a 360° scene

Turn around once, following the dots on screen. Your photos are stitched into a 360° panorama automatically.

  1. Hold the phone upright and use the normal 1× lens
  2. Stay on one spot and turn around the phone, not your body
  3. Line up with a dot and pause — the photo is taken for you
  4. Keep the lighting the same and avoid people walking past

Takes about 2–3 minutes, 38 photos.
